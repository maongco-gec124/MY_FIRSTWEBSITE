THE STORY BEHIND HABI//LOCAL

MADE
WITH
STYLE.

HABI//LOCAL is a personal fashion project centered around Filipino local clothing brands. It brings together different labels, styles, and creative identities in one space, highlighting pieces that deserve to be discovered. From everyday essentials to streetwear and statement pieces, HABI//LOCAL is a curated look into the diversity and creativity of local Filipino fashion.

02 / ABOUT THE CREATOR

ABOUT
THE CREATOR

Hi, I’m Shianmar, a 22-year-old who has loved and appreciated fashion since 2020. Over the years, I’ve come across different styles and clothing brands that caught my attention, especially local Filipino brands that have their own identity and way of expressing creativity through fashion.

I created HABI//LOCAL as a way to share some of the local brands I know and genuinely think are worth checking out. From everyday pieces to streetwear and more distinct styles, this website is simply my little collection of brands that I think deserve more attention and appreciation.

For me, fashion is not just about what is trending. It is also about discovering pieces that feel personal, supporting local creativity, and appreciating the people behind the clothes we wear.

03 / PURPOSE

WHY I MADE THIS

FASHION IS
EXPRESSION.

Fashion is more than just what we wear. It can reflect personality, creativity, culture, and individuality. Through HABI//LOCAL, I wanted to create something that reflects my own interest in fashion while giving attention to the creativity of Filipino local brands.
